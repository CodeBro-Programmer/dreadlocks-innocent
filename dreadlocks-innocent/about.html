About Us

Built on patience, grown into a studio.

Dreadlocks Innocent started with a simple belief: locs done right take time, and clients deserve a stylist who won't rush that process.

Our Story

From one chair to a full studio

What began as loc maintenance for friends and family in Port Harcourt grew, one referral at a time, into Dreadlocks Innocent, a studio in Mgbuoba dedicated entirely to locs: starting them, maintaining them, repairing them, and styling them for whatever comes next.

We work with every hair type and every stage of the loc journey from a first-time starter set to locs that have matured for years and simply need a confident retwist. Healthy scalp, healthy hair, and a look you're proud of: that's the standard for every chair in the studio.
