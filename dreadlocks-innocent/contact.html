Frequently Asked

Questions we hear a lot

A standard root retwist usually takes 1 – 2 hours, depending on how much hair has grown and how many locs you have. We'll give you a clearer estimate when you book.

Most clients come in for a retwist every 4 – 8 weeks. Newer locs often need more frequent visits, while mature locs can usually stretch a little longer between appointments.

We strongly recommend booking ahead so we can set aside enough time for your service. You can book online or message us on WhatsApp.

Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows, but a booked appointment guarantees your spot — especially on weekends, which tend to fill up quickly.

We use residue-free, loc-friendly washes and light locking gels suited to your hair type — never anything heavy or wax-based that causes build-up over time.
